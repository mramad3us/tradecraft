## The career into the deep postings (`src/sim/postings.js`)

**What a posting's end offers** (offers(); the result paper lists them, the closed ones with why). A posting done well (completed, or a first tour's promotion):
- **A station under official cover** (a handler's), every station, as before. After a deep post that ended badly (the job lost, brought out hunted, out of a prison) it's the only kind offered: back on the official ladder.
- **An undercover post**, in a city whose station has legends to be had (`src/sim/legends.js` DEEP: Moscow, Beirut, Cairo; never London), after a handler's posting carried out, or for a legend straight from London's first tour (the user: "they are allowed to go from London starting posting to undercover, then illegal").
- **An illegal's post**: Tradecraft ILLEGAL_OS (80) and an undercover post carried out to its end (the user: "only available for 80+ tradecraft officers who succesfully achieved a full undercover assignment"): not begun, not lost, not sacked from.

**A face on file** (facesOf(); the user, 2026-11-04: "if our face is in GRU/FSB files as a diplomatic employee, no amount of time will suffice to go unnoticed as an illegal or undercover under an other legend"). Every country served in under official cover (a first tour, a handler's posting) is closed to the deep posts for the rest of the career; so is a country where a first tour's trip abroad was made (burned, made, blown: their service filed a face off a diplomatic passport), and one where a legend was blown. The faces ride with the career from posting to posting (c.faces). My call: a deep post is also closed where you've lived another legend already (servedDeep(): the same face under two names in one country).

**The end of a career** (careerEnd()). Past RETIRE_AGE (60) at a posting's end, never in the middle of one (the user: "Officers retire when they cross 60 years old and reach the end of a posting (they don't retire mid-post)"). A legend (the picker's fourth file) has LEGEND_AFTER = 2 postings past the one they start in, then stops with a pretext (PRETEXTS: the scan, the heart, the diagnosis kept a year; the user: "the already old officer should have a pretext to retire (can be terminal disease) after two postings at most"). An illegal's post is the last: nothing is offered after it; brought out alive, the career's over, and a good end.

**The next posting** (nextPosting()). Official: as before (sim/campaign.js promote(): the leave, the language to working). Deep: the training (sim/career.js plan(): the leave, the language to fluent undercover or native for an illegal, the skills to the post's floors, FLOORS, all put on the sheet: trained()), then the incubation (below), day one the Monday after it. The career's file keeps each phase (a posting and how it ended, the training, the legend lived) and the sheet shows them under Before.
- **The training's floors**: an undercover officer's FLOORS.undercover (Deception 14, Awareness 13, Tracking, Pathfinding and Social 12: a year and a little more of it for a handler, nothing for a legend); an illegal's as planned in 0.1.25 (the core four to 20, close combat, fitness and the gun to 18, the rest to 14).
- **The language**: an illegal is native (the user, 2026-10-26: "no accent, nothing"): NEED.illegal 'native', the last step NATIVE_K (1) of the language's FSI weeks more, "at the Institute, then a year and more abroad among its speakers, under a name not your own". So a regular officer's Arabic to native is the best part of five years.

**The rungs** (sim/campaign.js LEVELS): a first tour, a posting of your own, under a legend, an illegal. As before, a rung opens as a start once a game of yours has reached the one above it: carrying out an undercover post reaches the illegal's rung, which opens starting under a legend; the illegal's (the top) opens only in debug mode. A deep start (the picker's) is a handler's file aged on (Tradecraft DEEP_OS: 55–69 undercover, 80–88 an illegal), trained to the post's floors, with a past of its own (sim/legends.js backstory(): a handler's posting in another country, an illegal's an undercover post too, the training, the legend lived); the picker offers only the cities with legends.
