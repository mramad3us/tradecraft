# 0.2 changes (branch v0.2-postings)

Player-facing notes for the deep postings, in CHANGELOG style, to fold into CHANGELOG.md at the merge. The design is docs/POSTINGS.md.

## The career into the deep postings

- A posting done well now offers more than a station: **an undercover post** in Moscow, Beirut or Cairo (never London), after a handler's posting carried out, and **an illegal's post** for Tradecraft 80 and an undercover post carried out to its end. The result paper lists every offer, with how long it takes to get there, and why the closed ones are closed.
- **A face on file**: every country you've served in under official cover is closed to you undercover or as an illegal, for good; so is one where a first tour's trip got you made, or a legend got blown.
- **The training**: an undercover post wants its language fluent, an illegal's native (a year and more among its speakers), and the skills to the post's floors; the orders paper says each phase and who teaches it.
- **The incubation**: before day one, the legend is lived: 3 to 20 months undercover, 3 to 14 years as an illegal, by how hard the place is to get into and how good the country's counter-intelligence is. The years go by (the date, your age), and the legend's history is on your file (C): the job since when, the flat, the café; for an illegal, perhaps a marriage and a child who never know. Lying low that long leaves the street's craft rusty.
- **The rungs**: "Under a legend" and "An illegal" join the picker's start. Carrying out an undercover post opens starting under a legend; an illegal's start is debug's.
- **Retiring**: past 60 at a posting's end; a legend after two postings past the one they start in, with a reason to stop at last. An illegal's post is the last there is.
- **The long rivalries**: a deep post's purpose is a thread of a decades-long rivalry (the long war with Moscow since 1947; with Tehran by way of the party since 1983; Moscow's long courtship of Cairo since 1955), not the station's business of the season.

## Under a legend: the undercover posting

- No embassy: the workplace is the employer's door on the posting's streets, its plate over it; the day's work is done from its door (T), nine to six, no arch, no Marine. The job's standing is your employer's view of you (the mind's and the sheet's words say so). Lose the job and the posting's lost (LET GO).
- Heat starts at nothing, and nobody watches a clean legend.
- **No immunity**: arrested, the legend is looked into (your Deception against their counter-intelligence, harder the graver the charge, easier the longer the legend's been lived). It holds: a brawl is 48 hours and bail, the posting going on; a gun is years (served under the legend's name, part of it, then a deal nobody signs), the posting over. It breaks: their prison (Lefortovo, Tora, a basement in the Dahiyeh), and the career's over. A weapon found at a police stop is an arrest now, and so is the black market's sting.
- **Blown is hunted**: at the top of the heat there's nobody to expel you; the city hunts a foreign spy for days, your face theirs, your flat theirs (they wait on the stairs).
- **The way out is your own**: no agency extraction; on the phone leaf, the way out (only hunted or blown): a place far from the centre, open six hours, by your own means. Reached, you're out, alive and burned.
- **The station by dead drop**: the week's review comes as the station's word behind a bench on Monday morning, a chalk mark telling you where; the drops are on the mind's map now.
- Agency support 40 at most, back 4 a day.

## The illegal's posting

- Activated by the Center's signal after years lying low: a citizen's name and papers, native, Tradecraft 80 and more, no embassy at all; a handler who's a retired illegal, by signal twice a year. Agency support 20 at most, back 2 a day.
- **The last posting**: no "posting complete". Each tasking done, the Center's next, left at a drop (the long rivalry's thread open then, or its next question); a job lost, another found. It runs till they take you, you die, or the Center brings you out.
- **Brought out**: almost impossible. Hunted or blown, a signal to the Center first and a day or two for its answer; then a place far out of the city, open three hours. Reached, the career's over, and well.

## Advanced disguises

- From a handler's posting on (with a network to run), the phone leaf offers the agency's disguise officer: three faces made for you (fifteen years older, a heavier build, another face) and kept at a safe house.
- At its door, T: the card, and half an hour at the mirror. What a witness says of you is someone else's: another age, another build, another face. Off at its door, it's kept; off anywhere else, it's gone.
- How good it is depends on the disguise officer and your Deception; a good eye reads through it (a patrolman seldom, an expert often), and then it's worse than nothing.

## The long rivalries

- A deep post's brief now says what the long war did while you were away ("The long war, meanwhile"), beside the station's news; the sheet's Purpose says how long it's run.
- An illegal's taskings follow the rivalry over the decades: the same thread's next question while it runs, the next thread when it turns.
